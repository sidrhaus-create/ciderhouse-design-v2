# Project Brief

## Project
Full digital rebrand and redevelopment of `ciderhouse.ru`.

## Goal
Create a premium, modern, multi-page brand platform that presents Cider House as a category-leading contemporary beverage producer. The experience should be visually memorable, dynamic, mobile-first, and easy to maintain.

## Existing content areas to migrate and improve
- Home
- Product assortment/catalog
- Production
- Where to buy
- Company/about
- Partners
- News
- Contacts
- Merch
- FAQ
- Age gate and legal content
- 0% collection

## Brand/product families
- Cider House master brand
- Double Tree
- White Phoenix
- Mister Bee
- White Phoenix 0% / non-alcoholic range
- Future cans and lemonades

## Key design idea
One unified digital system with a distinct visual world for each product family. Typography, grid, navigation, UI behavior, accessibility, and product-lock rules remain consistent across the site.

## Experience principles
1. Product is the hero.
2. Motion tells a story; it is not decoration.
3. Mobile is designed independently where needed.
4. Real product assets are never regenerated.
5. Every page has a clear conversion purpose.
6. Content must be editable without rewriting components.
7. The user must always retain normal scrolling control.

## Primary audiences
- End consumers discovering flavors and availability
- Existing fans
- Retail and distribution partners
- HoReCa
- Event/festival visitors
- Candidates and business contacts

## Primary actions
- Explore product ranges
- Find a specific product
- Locate a store
- Visit approved marketplace links for non-alcoholic products
- Submit a partnership request
- Read production and brand information
- Follow social channels
